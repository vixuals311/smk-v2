## Notes

- Out compensation now works for ASCII text: every letter, word and line of the tested cases is blank on frame 119.
- The one remaining timing inaccuracy is non-ASCII text (accents, emoji): the Out starts (bytes − characters) × per-character delay too early because `#text` counts bytes. For "éüÈ ÀBC" that is 4 frames. The last-frame alpha is still 0.00. A fix would be to count characters with `utf8.len` or by pattern matching on UTF-8 lead bytes in the amount expression (not tried).
- Bounce on the Letter macro visibly bounces as designed (the largest end-of-In step, 11 px).
- Not tested: ExtraBold / SemiBold / italic weights, the real look of the emoji glyph, `SMK2_Motion` / `SMK2_MotionRig` smoke test.
